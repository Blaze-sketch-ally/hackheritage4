# Problem Statement

Academia and industry operate largely in isolation: students lack visibility
into the skills industry actually needs, faculty and institutions lack
structured channels for industry collaboration, and industry struggles to
find and validate job-ready talent. AIC Portal aims to connect
Student, Faculty, Institution, and Industry on one platform, driven by a
skill-first workflow (assessment -> gap analysis -> learning -> portfolio ->
matching -> placement -> analytics).
